01 / The question

How has Earth's temperature changed?

NASA measures global surface temperature anomalies: the difference between a given year's temperature and the 1951–1980 average. Looking at the record from 1880 through 2025 reveals a persistent warming trend, despite year-to-year variability.

The central finding: The warming is not just a handful of unusually hot years. It becomes visible in the annual record, the changing balance of cool and warm years, and the average temperature of entire decades.

03 / The takeaway

Different charts. The same signal.

The annual chart shows the timing of warming. The stripes make the transition from cooler to warmer years intuitive. The decade bars demonstrate that the change persists when years are grouped together. Taken together, they tell a consistent story of long-term global surface warming.
